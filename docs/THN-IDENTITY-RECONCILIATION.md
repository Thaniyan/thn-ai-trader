## Working interaction

The market hero uses the existing catalog and context selector. Supported quick
choices delegate to the same original instrument action; they are not a second
catalog or a live-price strip. On phones these choices stay inside the existing
keyboard-accessible chooser, keeping the opening chart discoverable. The source
certificate remains available below the chart and through the original evidence
navigation, instead of occupying a second hero.

The Decision Center identifies the current instrument/timeframe and existing
research state. It prominently labels deterministic rules and inactive hosted
model generation. Conditional and Eligible remain research-only; WAIT does not
release entry/stop/target levels. The original candidate, identity and risk gates
remain authoritative. The native chart object, coordinates, drawing controls,
source candle inspection and TradingView lifecycle are reused.

The Trade Ticket groups the retained entry/stop and three independent targets.
Focusing or selecting a recorded level explains its actual role and uses the
original coordinate-linked inspection. Entry is not an executable quote; gaps
and slippage can exceed a stop; targets are not partial-exit allocation or promised
return. No position size, probability, price zone, return or risk formula is
calculated by the presentation module. Explanations are removed when context
changes or the original candidate becomes unavailable. "Why these levels?" opens
the existing source/methodology review and restores keyboard focus on Escape.

Risk assumptions, explicit validation, notes and saving continue through the
single original planner and Library owners. The original records are never
silently edited or re-evaluated. Heuristic evidence, source reliability, historical
fixed-rule requirements and unavailable calibrated win probability stay separate.
