# Product vision

THN AI Trader is a bilingual, non-custodial market-analysis and paper-trading
workstation. Its purpose is reliable data, explainable decisions and disciplined
risk review. It is not a broker, investment adviser or validated profit predictor.

Preserve Dashboard, Markets, Trading Terminal, AI Intelligence, Risk Desk,
Portfolio, Trade Journal, Broker Accounts and Settings in English and Arabic.
Every quote needs identity, source and age; every analytical limitation needs an
honest unavailable/degraded state. A blank value is preferable to invented evidence.

Success means observable provider reliability, understandable indicator reasoning,
server-enforced paper risk, reconciled ledgers, usable mobile/RTL layouts and
measurable accessibility. AI narrative adds explanation; it cannot change numeric
levels or authorize execution. Historical strategy evaluation is a roadmap item,
not a capability inferred from confidence scores. Real-money execution is excluded.

The original repository is the intended future source of truth. Independent
protected staging and explicit production promotion keep experimentation away
from the existing production website. This local reconciliation activates neither.
