## A connected research workflow

The actual Command Center now has a unified Research Assurance workspace with five
working steps: explain confluence, inspect the hypothetical scenario, test explicit
fixed rules, validate risk assumptions and record the research thesis. Native
keyboard disclosures keep deep evidence out of the opening chart. Escape restores
focus to the initiating review action. The original chart/controller, notes,
Library and Risk Desk remain authoritative.

Four deliberately separate indicators identify heuristic school agreement,
historical requirement outcomes, selected-context reliability and unavailable
calibrated win probability. The legacy circular Dashboard gauge becomes a compact
numeric context badge. A count of passed historical requirements is labeled as
such, never a trade success rate. Existing score contracts are preserved; no new
score, threshold or financial formula is introduced.

The confluence explanation states the original clamp/weighting rule and copies
recorded weighted consensus and directional school count. The original response
does not retain its risk-warning count separately from merged/truncated warnings;
that component is explicitly unavailable rather than reconstructed. Each school
retains its direction and recorded reason, including opposition and neutrality,
and links to the existing complete evidence ledger. Arabic controls, labels,
methodology and school names are localized. Verbatim engine reasons retain their
original English, explicitly labeled in Arabic; no invented translation or
model-generated explanation replaces source evidence.

The candidate review copies the exact retained E/SL/T1–T3 and explains their roles
and limitations. It links to the original swing/ATR/target methodology, conditional
confirmation/invalidation, supporting/opposing evidence and original planner.
Missing, stale, conflicting, proxy, currency or contract evidence can suppress a
candidate; the review shows blockers and does not populate plausible fake levels.
Conditional hypotheses remain distinct from an eligible research candidate.

The original risk planner requires explicit user assumptions and authoritative
validation. The review calculates no position size, R/R or return. Preview leverage
is hypothetical, account leverage is unchanged, execution may differ from a
hypothetical entry and hosted execution stays unavailable. Changing risk assumptions
does not silently change historical strategy results.

## Historical testing without a parallel evaluator

Opening the inline test moves the **single original** historical form into the
Command Center using retained DOM anchors. It changes no settings and sends no
request. Explicit submission still uses the original evaluator and API, including
its running/dirty/failed/context invalidation rules. Returning to Historical Lab
restores the same form node and settings. No duplicate form, endpoint, financial
engine, saved-record schema or migration is added.

The **single original** validation report and ten-check explanation move alongside
the review when appropriate. Its existing explanations, assumptions navigation,
periods, costs, stress variants, walk-forward evidence and precision rules remain
intact. A separate read-only summary copies actual development and chronological
holdout metrics and exact evaluation symbol/interval/hash. Absent values are not
zero; a changed/running/failed report is retained historical evidence, never current
validation. A different symbol/interval/hash has no matching results.

This evaluator tests selected fixed-strategy rules, **not** the heuristic school
model and **not** a single proposed trade. It does not establish predictive
calibration. Reused holdouts, small samples, assumed fills/costs and missing metrics
remain explicit limitations. Legacy simulated analysis backtests are not substituted
for authoritative evaluation outputs.
