## Significant functional and visible changes

- Browser research replay is integrated into Library → Open report. It verifies the retained OHLCV SHA-256 locally and on the existing server, then reuses the authoritative evaluator. Offline CLI and browser HTTP replay delegate to the same service. It never refetches provider candles or changes account state. Results distinguish reproduced, numerical mismatch, cannot reproduce and integrity failure, with exact recorded-field/boundary deltas, stored/recomputed metrics, parameters/windows and computation limits.
- Replay handles missing snapshots, unsupported versions, malformed/oversized records, invalid parameters, mismatched identities, tampering and interrupted requests explicitly. Numerical reproduction never authenticates an imported report or overwrites its original outcomes. Local reproduction stamps are supplemental and are cleared as trusted status on import. Stale/disposed/cancelled responses cannot update newer UI.
- Retained verified candles can open a separate read-only historical pane using the original chart component and authoritative server EMA/RSI/ATR/structure formulas. Current observations remain separate and explicitly labeled. Pan/zoom, keyboard inspection and structure overlays are preserved; the replay response includes at most 200 recent causal events. A report's selection or Copilot frame claim can open the current chart, with exact hash-match/mismatch disclosure. Hypothetical plan overlays have a visibility toggle.
- Quantitative comparison supports two to four cases: instrument/timeframe, provider/hash, coverage/windows, rules/direction/parameters/costs, development and holdout return, drawdown, trades, exposure, defined profit factor/mean R, sensitivity and walk-forward summaries. Incompatible data/windows/engines, imported evidence, sample uncertainty and gaps are flagged. Signed aggregate-return bars represent separate recorded windows, never simultaneous equity paths. Descriptive deltas do not rank a winner or assert significance.
- Markets gains accessible curated discovery across 18 mappings/five categories, explicit candidate-versus-observed coverage, consistent selection and direct chart access. Dashboard includes selected observed close, previous-observed-candle change with actual timestamps, EMA/ATR context, recency and a time-spaced history mini-chart. Accepted shared history is reused for the selected focus rather than reverting to an unavailable six-watchlist quote.
- Data Quality distinguishes API health, provider availability, stale observations, insufficient history and cadence mismatch. Requested/actual interval, source/provider symbol, currency, times/count/coverage, hash, gaps, omitted rows, volume and proxies are inspectable. Retries are explicit/bounded. No streaming or continuous cloud monitoring is claimed.
- Corrected `NAS100` from Yahoo `^IXIC` (Nasdaq Composite) to `^NDX` (Nasdaq-100). A genuine Yahoo request returned 887 closed hourly USD observations for `^NDX`. Existing saved data keeps its original provider identity; no old report/snapshot is silently reinterpreted or rewritten.
- Risk planning reuses authoritative contract rules for mapped USD-supported instruments, explicitly flags cost-adjusted exposure above nominal budget or margin above capital, and visualizes independent saved-plan stop exposure. Native USDT is not treated as USD. Plans remain hypothetical, never executable orders or fake broker balances.
- EN/AR/RTL, responsive dense tables and terminal typography/palette are retained/refined. The tablet overflow discovered in Markets/Risk/Library was corrected with contained horizontal table scrolling rather than clipped content. Overflow tables gain descriptive keyboard focus when entering a view or opening disclosure. Existing nine sections, drawings, SMC maps, indicators, experiments, notebook, backup/recovery and isolated local paper code remain.

## Bounds and remaining limitations

Replay sends the saved research and snapshot to a same-origin read-only compute endpoint. Limits are 1 MB UTF-8 input, 2,000 candles, bounded metric shapes, 18 engine runs and two simultaneous replay requests per server process. No platform-wide rate limiter or persistent queue is added. Computation already submitted may finish after client cancellation; its response is ignored when stale. No submitted dataset is persisted or logged by the application.

Supported evaluator version is `6.3`; known legacy v6.4–v6.6 product reports may use that engine. Unknown numerical engines are refused. Reproduction compares recorded metric fields and chronology, not missing/unrecorded fields or provider authenticity. New reports retain actual development-first/holdout-last times and RSI/ATR period parameters; legacy reports without an actual end show it as unrecorded. Strategy formulas and conservative next-open/stop-first/gap execution semantics are unchanged.

Browser-local storage remains unencrypted, device-local, finite and removable: 24 reports, 120 bounded legacy archives, two retained snapshots and 1.8 MB content. Physical quotas may be lower. Backup import is bounded to 2.1 MB and previews append-only restoration; unreadable/stale/quota conditions preserve previous work. Recomputed tables/charts are session results with only supplemental local status metadata retained; imports cannot carry trusted reproduction stamps. Optional tags/folders and encrypted backups were not added.

A hash does not promise future retrieval or authenticate a provider. Without retained raw candles, replay is impossible and fresh data is never substituted. Only the selected timeframe is retained, other frames remain summarized; historical-chart structure events are capped at the latest 200. Old `NAS100` records using Composite `^IXIC` retain that disclosed source, rather than becoming Nasdaq-100 records after the mapping correction.

Coverage varies by provider/instrument/interval. Curated mappings are not guarantees of availability. Recency is heuristic rather than a verified exchange-session calendar. Forex volume may be absent; metals/oil are futures proxies; historical revisions, omitted rows, session/data gaps and corporate actions limit inference. No news, verified calendar, sentiment, future win probability, significance or guaranteed profitability is fabricated. There is no new optimizer or AI narrative.

USD planning is hypothetical, contract terms unverified and broker execution disabled. Stops do not guarantee fills; costs/gaps can exceed nominal budgets. Financing, borrowing, actual short availability and forced liquidation remain unmodeled. Target outcomes are independent full-position alternatives, not partial allocations or an aggregate portfolio. Current and retained charts are intentionally labeled as separate data contexts.

These are local candidate results, not Production-browser verification. No v6.6 push or deployment occurred in this cycle.
